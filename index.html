FAQ

Questions, answered.

Do I buy a website or a subscription? +

Thimmo WebForge templates are presented as one-time website purchases. Hosting, domains and third-party services can be handled separately.

Can I request changes? +

Yes. Send your business information and the changes you need. Custom work can be quoted separately.

Can I use the website for my business? +

Yes. After the purchase/customization process, the website can be prepared for the buyer's business. Your own sales terms should define exactly what is included.

How do I pay? +

This storefront uses an order-request flow. Connect the order button to your preferred payment provider or WhatsApp when you are ready to accept payments online.
